eSIM for United Kingdom

Travelling to United Kingdom? An eSIM can be a simple way to get mobile data without hunting for a physical SIM after arrival.

What to check before buying

  • Your phone supports eSIM.
  • Your device is carrier-unlocked.
  • The plan explicitly includes coverage in United Kingdom.
  • The data allowance matches the length of your trip.
  • You understand when the plan activates.

How much data do you need?

Light messaging and maps usually need much less data than video, social media uploads or hotspot use. Choosing a smaller plan first can be cheaper than automatically buying the largest package.
