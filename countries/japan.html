What uses data while travelling?

  • Maps and navigation throughout the day
  • Train and route searches
  • Translation and web searches
  • Messaging and photo sharing
  • Social media
  • Video and cloud backups
  • Hotspot use for another device

Video, automatic photo backups and hotspot use can increase consumption much faster than maps or messaging.

Before buying a Japan eSIM

  • Check that your phone supports eSIM.
  • Make sure the device is carrier-unlocked.
  • Check when the plan starts: installation or first network connection.
  • Check whether tethering or hotspot use is allowed if you need it.
  • Keep the installation details accessible before you travel.

Country plan or regional Asia plan?

If Japan is your only destination, a Japan-specific plan may be simpler. If your trip includes several countries, compare it with a regional plan so you do not have to switch connectivity during the trip.

The calculator is an estimate, not a measurement of actual future use. Your consumption depends on apps, settings and network behaviour.
